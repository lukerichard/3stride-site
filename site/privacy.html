Privacy

How we handle your information

Last updated November 27, 2026

3-Stride connects companies that need parts made with machine shops in Ontario. This page explains what information we collect when you visit our website or send us a request, what we do with it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • What you send us. Your name, company, email, phone number, whether you are a buyer or a shop, your message, and any drawings or CAD files you attach.
  • Visit information. We use Apollo.io's website tracker, which uses cookies and your IP address to record pages visited and, where possible, the company a visit comes from.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and find shops that can quote your job.
  • To understand which companies are interested in our service so we can follow up.
  • To keep business records.

We do not sell your information.

Your drawings

Drawings and CAD files stay confidential. We only share them with the shops we ask to quote your job, and only for that purpose.

Where your information is stored

We use a few trusted service providers to run this site and our business:

  • Google stores form submissions and uploaded files.
  • Apollo.io provides visitor tracking.
  • Netlify hosts this website.

These providers may store information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

How long we keep it

We keep request information and files for as long as we need them to serve you and keep business records. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.

Contact

Questions or requests about your information: hello@3-stride.com
